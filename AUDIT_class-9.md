## 3. Notable Items & Syllabus Nuances (Audit Watchlist)

The following items are completely defensible and mathematically/historically precise, but have subtle nuances that teachers and students should note:

1. **`c9-gk-005` (Highest Mountain Peak in India)**:
   - *Question Context*: Kangchenjunga (8,586 m) in Sikkim is the highest mountain peak in India (and 3rd highest worldwide). Note: Nanda Devi (7,816 m in Uttarakhand) is the highest peak located *entirely* within undivided Indian territory without crossing international boundaries. Both distinctions are explicitly preserved in the stem and explanation.
2. **`c9-gk-040` (Elevation of Mount Everest)**:
   - *Question Context*: Mount Everest was jointly remeasured by Nepal and China in December 2020 at 8,848.86 metres. The answer accepts 8,849 m with a tolerance of ±1 m (accepting 8,848 m as well).
3. **`c9-gk-059` (Founding of the Indian National Army)**:
   - *Historical Note*: Azad Hind Fauj was initially conceptualized and formed in 1942 in Malaya by Captain Mohan Singh with British-Indian POWs. Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose took over its supreme leadership in Singapore in July 1943. The question tests this distinction.
4. **`c9-gk-064` (Number of States in the Indian Union)**:
   - *Administrative Fact*: India currently comprises 28 States and 8 Union Territories following the Jammu & Kashmir Reorganisation Act (2019) and the merger of Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu (2020).
5. **`c9-biology-072` (Diversity in Living Organisms)**:
   - *Curriculum Note*: NCERT rationalized some sections of "Diversity in Living Organisms" in recent post-2022 trimmed booklets; however, competitive foundation syllabi (Olympiads, NTSE, NEET foundation) retain the Five Kingdom classification and plant/animal taxonomy. All questions adhere strictly to standard NCERT textbook concepts.
6. **`c9-chemistry-067` (Rutherford Scattering Angles)**:
   - *Calculation Context*: The ratio of gold nucleus radius (~10^-15 m) to atom radius (~10^-10 m) gives a volumetric factor of ~10^15; approximately 1 in 20,000 alpha particles rebound at >90°. The explanation provides the exact experimental observation.
7. **`c9-cs-015` (Number System - Binary Conversions)**:
   - *Pedagogical Note*: Binary to decimal, octal, and hexadecimal conversions use exact positional weights (powers of 2), with step-by-step arithmetic shown in all explanations.
8. **`c9-maths-089` (Heron's Formula & Radicals)**:
   - *Accuracy Note*: Exact square roots and integer area solutions (e.g. triangles with side lengths 13, 14, 15 having area 84 cm²) were deliberately chosen over irrational rounding to ensure unequivocal numerical grading.
