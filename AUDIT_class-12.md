## 2. Curriculum Coverage & Chapters Verified

1. **Mathematics (`maths.json`)**:
   - *Relations and Functions*: Equivalence relations, reflexive, symmetric, transitive, bijective functions, composition and inverse functions.
   - *Inverse Trigonometric Functions*: Principal value branches, domain, range, properties and identities.
   - *Matrices*: Order, operations (addition, scalar multiplication, multiplication), transpose, symmetric and skew-symmetric matrices, elementary row operations, invertible matrices.
   - *Determinants*: Properties of determinants, minors, cofactors, adjoint, matrix inverse ($A^{-1} = \frac{1}{|A|}\text{adj}(A)$), solving systems of linear equations using matrix method (Cramer's rule/inversion).
   - *Continuity and Differentiability*: Continuity of functions, differentiability, chain rule, derivatives of inverse trigonometric and implicit functions, logarithmic differentiation, parametric differentiation, second order derivatives.
   - *Application of Derivatives*: Rate of change of quantities, increasing and decreasing functions, tangents and normals, approximations, maxima and minima (first and second derivative tests).
   - *Integrals*: Indefinite integrals (substitution, partial fractions, integration by parts), definite integrals as limit of a sum, Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, properties of definite integrals.
   - *Application of Integrals*: Area under simple curves (lines, circles, parabolas, ellipses), area bounded between two curves.
   - *Differential Equations*: Order and degree, formation, solution by variable separable method, homogeneous differential equations, first order linear differential equations ($dy/dx + Py = Q$).
   - *Vector Algebra*: Vectors and scalars, magnitude and direction cosines/ratios, dot (scalar) product, cross (vector) product, projection of a vector.
   - *Three-Dimensional Geometry*: Direction cosines and direction ratios, equation of a line in vector and Cartesian form, angle between two lines, shortest distance between skew lines, plane equations, coplanarity.
   - *Linear Programming*: Formulation of LPP, graphical method of solution in two variables, feasible and infeasible regions, bounded and unbounded optimal solutions.
   - *Probability*: Conditional probability, multiplication theorem, independent events, total probability theorem, Bayes' Theorem, random variables, probability distribution.

2. **Physics (`physics.json`)**:
   - *Electric Charges and Fields*: Coulomb's law, electric field, electric field lines, electric dipole, torque on a dipole in uniform field, electric flux, Gauss's law and its applications (infinite wire, infinite sheet, spherical shell).
   - *Electrostatic Potential and Capacitance*: Electric potential, potential due to a point charge and dipole, equipotential surfaces, electrical potential energy, conductors and dielectrics, capacitance of a parallel plate capacitor (with and without dielectric medium), combination of capacitors, energy stored in a capacitor.
   - *Current Electricity*: Electric current, drift velocity, Ohm's law, electrical resistance and resistivity, temperature dependence of resistance, internal resistance of a cell, combination of cells, Kirchhoff's rules, Wheatstone bridge, metre bridge, potentiometer principle and applications.
   - *Moving Charges and Magnetism*: Biot-Savart law and applications (circular loop), Ampere's circuital law (infinite wire, solenoid, toroid), force on a moving charge in magnetic field (Lorentz force), cyclotron, force on current-carrying conductor, force between parallel currents, torque on magnetic dipole, moving coil galvanometer and conversion to ammeter/voltmeter.
   - *Magnetism and Matter*: Bar magnet as equivalent solenoid, magnetic field lines, Earth's magnetic field and magnetic elements, magnetic properties of materials (diamagnetic, paramagnetic, ferromagnetic), hysteresis loop.
   - *Electromagnetic Induction*: Magnetic flux, Faraday's laws of induction, Lenz's law, motional electromotive force, eddy currents, self-induction and mutual induction, AC generator.
   - *Alternating Current*: Peak and RMS value of AC, AC through resistor, inductor, capacitor, series LCR circuit, resonance, quality factor ($Q$), power in AC circuits (power factor), wattless current, transformers.
   - *Electromagnetic Waves*: Displacement current, Maxwell's equations, characteristics of EM waves, electromagnetic spectrum (radio, micro, infrared, visible, ultraviolet, X-rays, gamma rays) and applications.
   - *Ray Optics and Optical Instruments*: Reflection and refraction at spherical surfaces, total internal reflection, lens maker's formula, combination of thin lenses, prism dispersion, optical instruments (compound microscope, astronomical telescope).
   - *Wave Optics*: Huygens' principle, reflection and refraction of plane waves, interference of light, Young's double slit experiment, fringe width, diffraction of light through single slit, resolution of optical instruments, polarisation and Brewster's law.
   - *Dual Nature of Radiation and Matter*: Photoelectric effect, Hertz and Lenard's observations, Einstein's photoelectric equation, de Broglie hypothesis, Davisson-Germer experiment.
   - *Atoms*: Alpha-particle scattering experiment, Rutherford's atomic model, Bohr model of hydrogen atom, energy levels and line spectra of hydrogen atom.
   - *Nuclei*: Composition and size of nucleus, atomic masses, mass defect, binding energy per nucleon and its variation with mass number, nuclear fission and fusion, radioactive decay law, half-life, alpha/beta/gamma decay.
   - *Semiconductor Electronics*: Energy bands in conductors, semiconductors and insulators, intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors (n-type, p-type), p-n junction diode (forward and reverse bias), I-V characteristics, diode as a rectifier (half-wave and full-wave), special purpose diodes (Zener diode as voltage regulator, LED, photodiode, solar cell), digital logic gates.

   - *Solutions*: Types of solutions, expression of concentration, solubility of gases in liquids (Henry's law), vapour pressure of liquid solutions, Raoult's law, ideal and non-ideal solutions, colligative properties (relative lowering of vapour pressure, elevation of boiling point, depression of freezing point, osmotic pressure), abnormal molar mass and van 't Hoff factor ($i$).
   - *Electrochemistry*: Redox reactions, electrochemical cells (galvanic and electrolytic), standard electrode potential, Nernst equation and its application to chemical cells, relation between Gibbs energy and EMF, conductance in electrolytic solutions (specific, molar conductivity), Kohlrausch's law of independent migration of ions, electrolysis (Faraday's laws), commercial batteries (primary, secondary, fuel cells), corrosion mechanism.
   - *Chemical Kinetics*: Rate of reaction (average and instantaneous), factors affecting rate, rate law and specific rate constant, order and molecularity of a reaction, integrated rate equations for zero and first order reactions, half-life of a reaction, temperature dependence of rate constant (Arrhenius equation, activation energy), collision theory of chemical reactions.
   - *d and f Block Elements*: Transition elements: electronic configuration, occurrence, general trends in properties (atomic and ionic radii, ionization enthalpies, oxidation states, magnetic properties, catalytic properties, formation of coloured ions, interstitial compounds, alloy formation), preparation and properties of $K_2Cr_2O_7$ and $KMnO_4$; Lanthanoids (lanthanoid contraction) and Actinoids.
   - *Coordination Compounds*: Werner's coordination theory, ligands, coordination number, oxidation number, IUPAC nomenclature of mononuclear coordination compounds, isomerism (structural and stereoisomerism), bonding in coordination compounds: Valence Bond Theory (inner and outer orbital complexes), Crystal Field Theory (crystal field splitting in octahedral and tetrahedral complexes, spectrochemical series), stability of coordination compounds, applications.
   - *Haloalkanes and Haloarenes*: Nomenclature, nature of C-X bond, methods of preparation, physical and chemical properties, mechanism of nucleophilic substitution ($S_N1$ and $S_N2$), stereochemistry of substitution reactions, optical activity, polyhalogen compounds (chloroform, iodoform, freons, DDT).
   - *Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers*: Nomenclature, methods of preparation, physical and chemical properties (acidic character of alcohols and phenols, esterification, oxidation, Kolbe's reaction, Reimer-Tiemann reaction), mechanism of dehydration of alcohols to alkenes, ethers: preparation (Williamson's synthesis) and cleavage of C-O bond.
   - *Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids*: Nomenclature, nature of carbonyl group, methods of preparation, physical properties, chemical properties: mechanism of nucleophilic addition, reactivity of aldehydes vs ketones, alpha-hydrogen reactions (aldol condensation, Cannizzaro reaction), oxidation and reduction reactions; Carboxylic acids: acidic strength, preparation, reactions.
   - *Amines (Organic Compounds Containing Nitrogen)*: Structure, classification, nomenclature, methods of preparation, physical and chemical properties, basic character of aliphatic and aromatic amines, distinction between primary, secondary, and tertiary amines (Hinsberg's test), diazotization, chemical reactions of diazonium salts and their synthetic importance.
   - *Biomolecules*: Carbohydrates (classification, monosaccharides: glucose and fructose, structure, Haworth projections, mutarotation, D-L configuration, oligosaccharides, polysaccharides: starch, cellulose, glycogen), Proteins (amino acids, peptide bond, polypeptides, primary/secondary/tertiary/quaternary structure, denaturation), Enzymes, Vitamins (classification and deficiency diseases), Nucleic acids (DNA and RNA structure, genetic code).
