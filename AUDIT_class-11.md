## 3. Notable Items & Syllabus Nuances (Class 11 vs Class 10 Depth)

1. **Chemistry: Dynamic Equilibrium & Thermodynamics**:
   - In Class 10, chemical reactions are taught qualitatively (reversible reactions, endothermic vs exothermic). In Class 11 Chemistry (`c11-chem-001` through `c11-chem-100`), questions rigorously test equilibrium constants $K_p$ and $K_c$ ($K_p = K_c(RT)^{\Delta n}$), Le Chatelier's principle shifts under temperature and partial pressure variations, buffer solution pH via the Henderson-Hasselbalch equation, and Gibbs free energy criteria ($\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S < 0$).
2. **Physics: Two-Dimensional Kinematics & Rotational Dynamics**:
   - While Class 10 Physics focuses on rectilinear motion and basic Ohm's law circuits, Class 11 Physics incorporates multi-step projectile motion kinematics ($R = \frac{u^2 \sin 2\theta}{g}$), rotational inertia ($I = \sum m_i r_i^2$), parallel and perpendicular axis theorems, and work-energy theorem derivations for variable spring forces.
3. **Mathematics: Permutations, Complex Numbers & Conic Sections**:
   - Questions involve advanced permutation arrangements with duplicate letters (e.g., arrangements of MISSISSIPPI: $\frac{11!}{4!4!2!} = 34650$), polar form of complex numbers, and standard locus definitions of conics (eccentricity of hyperbola $e = \sqrt{1 + b^2/a^2} > 1$).
4. **Biology: Molecular Cell Biology & Human Physiology Counter-Currents**:
   - Unlike Class 10's general overview of life processes, Class 11 questions evaluate the intricate mechanism of the counter-current multiplier in Henle's loop and vasa recta, the Z-scheme of non-cyclic photophosphorylation, Hatch-Slack $C_4$ dicarboxylic acid pathway, and sliding filament theory of sarcomere contraction.
5. **Computer Science: Python Memory Internals & Hardware Architecture**:
   - Class 11 CS moves beyond basic programming to address two's complement arithmetic, cache memory effective access time calculations ($T_{\text{eff}} = H \cdot T_c + (1-H)(T_c + T_m)$), LEGB variable scope resolution, list memory aliasing vs shallow copies, and cyber legislation under the IT Act 2000.
6. **General Knowledge: Constitutional Jurisprudence & Macroeconomics**:
   - Rather than basic civics (e.g. naming the President or Prime Minister), Class 11 GK tests Constitutional Articles (Articles 32, 40, 51A, 110, 280, 324, 352, 356, 368), Supreme Court doctrines (Basic Structure in Kesavananda Bharati 1973, Article 18 in Balaji Raghavan 1995), RBI quantitative monetary instruments (CRR, SLR, Repo rate transmission), and multilateral environmental treaties (Montreal Protocol Kigali Amendment, Basel, Stockholm, Minamata).
